# Comprehensive Design Prompt for Utility Warehouse Voice-First Onboarding Portal

**Project:** UW Partner Onboarding Portal Redesign — Voice-First Experience  

**For:** Emergent (Design Team)  

**Context:** Utility Warehouse is rebuilding their existing partner onboarding portal with a modern, voice-first approach using AI-powered training. The portal will onboard new UW Partners (independent distributors) through interactive modules, voice AI coaching, and milestone tracking.

---

## 🎯 **Design Brief Overview**

### **Primary Objective**

Create a modern, mobile-first landing page and dashboard experience that introduces new UW Partners to a revolutionary voice AI trainer. The design should feel **professional yet approachable**, **tech-forward but not intimidating**, and **aligned with Utility Warehouse's brand** while establishing its own identity as a premium training platform.

### **Target Audience**

- **New UW Partners**: Ages 25-55, varying tech literacy

- **Device Usage**: 70% mobile, 30% desktop

- **Mindset**: Excited but nervous about starting their UW journey

- **Goal**: Learn how to introduce UW services to friends, family, and community

- **Pain Points**: Overwhelmed by traditional training materials, need confidence before first customer conversation

### **Core Innovation**

**Voice-First AI Trainer**: A conversational AI coach (powered by Vapi) that partners can practice with anytime, anywhere. Think "personal sales mentor in your pocket."

## 🎨 **Design System & Brand Guidelines**

### **Color Palette**

**Primary (from UW brand):**

- UW Purple: `#9B59B6` (CTAs, headers, progress bars)

- UW Blue: `#3498DB` (secondary actions, links)

- UW Yellow/Gold: `#FFB921` (accents, highlights, success states)

**Neutrals:**

- Dark Gray: `#2C3E50` (body text)

- Medium Gray: `#7F8C8D` (secondary text)

- Light Gray: `#ECF0F1` (backgrounds, cards)

- White: `#FFFFFF` (content areas)

**System Colors:**

- Success: `#27AE60` (completed modules, unlocked)

- Warning: `#F39C12` (in progress)

- Error: `#E74C3C` (validation errors)

- Locked: `#BDC3C7` (locked modules)

---

### **Typography**

**Headings:**

- Font: DM Sans Bold (or similar modern sans-serif)

- H1: 48px / 56px (desktop / mobile), line-height 1.2

- H2: 36px / 32px, line-height 1.3

- H3: 24px / 20px, line-height 1.4

**Body:**

- Font: DM Sans Regular

- Body: 16px / 14px, line-height 1.6

- Small: 14px / 12px, line-height 1.5

**Accent (for AI transcription, code):**

- Font: DM Mono Medium

- Size: 14px, line-height 1.5

---

### **Iconography**

**Style:** Line icons with rounded corners (not sharp/angular)  

**Weight:** 2px stroke  

**Size:** 24px standard, 48px for feature icons  

**Source:** Lucide, Heroicons, or custom UW set  

**Key Icons Needed:**

- Microphone (voice AI)

- Lock/Unlock (module states)

- Checkmark (completed)

- Clock (duration)

- Play button (video)

- Download (templates)

- Calendar (mentor booking)

- Chart/Graph (progress)

---

### **Components**

**Buttons:**

- **Primary**: Purple fill, white text, 12px radius, hover: darken 10%

- **Secondary**: Purple outline, purple text, 12px radius, hover: light purple bg

- **Disabled**: Light gray fill, gray text, no hover

**Cards:**

- White background

- 16px radius corners

- Subtle shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`

- Hover: lift shadow to `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`

**Progress Bars:**

- Height: 8px

- Radius: 4px (pill shape)

- Fill: Purple gradient

- Background: Light gray

**Badges:**

- Pill shape (full radius)

- Small text (12px)

- Colors: Success (green), Warning (yellow), Locked (gray)

---

### **Spacing System**

Use 8px base unit:

- `xs`: 4px

- `sm`: 8px

- `md`: 16px

- `lg`: 24px

- `xl`: 32px

- `2xl`: 48px

---

### **Responsive Breakpoints**

- Mobile: 375px - 767px (design for iPhone SE minimum)

- Tablet: 768px - 1023px

- Desktop: 1024px - 1440px

- Large: 1441px+

**Critical:** Test on iPhone SE (375px), iPhone 14 Pro (393px), and common Androids

## 🚀 **Animation & Interactions**

### **Micro-interactions**

1. **Button Press:**

   - Scale: 0.98 on tap

   - Duration: 100ms

   - Ease: ease-out

2. **Card Hover (Desktop):**

   - Lift: translate Y -4px

   - Shadow increase

   - Duration: 200ms

3. **Module Unlock:**

   - Lock icon fades out

   - Card color shifts from gray to white

   - Gentle bounce animation

   - Duration: 400ms

4. **Progress Bar Fill:**

   - Animated on load (0% → actual %)

   - Duration: 800ms

   - Ease: ease-in-out

5. **Voice Waveform:**

   - Real-time frequency visualization

   - Smooth, organic movement (not rigid bars)

   - Color: Purple gradient

---

### **Page Transitions**

- Fade in/out: 300ms

- Slide transitions between modules: 400ms

- No jarring jumps or flashes

## ♿ **Accessibility Requirements**

### **Must-Haves**

1. **Color Contrast:**

   - All text: Minimum 4.5:1 ratio (WCAG AA)

   - Large text (24px+): Minimum 3:1 ratio

2. **Focus States:**

   - Visible outline on all interactive elements

   - Purple ring, 2px width, 4px offset

3. **Alt Text:**

   - All images and icons

   - Descriptive, not generic ("Module 3 thumbnail: person showing bill savings on phone" not "image")

4. **Keyboard Navigation:**

   - Tab order logical (top to bottom, left to right)

   - Enter/Space activates buttons

   - Escape closes modals

5. **Screen Reader Support:**

   - Semantic HTML (`<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`)

   - ARIA labels where needed

   - Live regions for AI transcription updates

6. **Voice AI Accessibility:**

   - Text transcript available for deaf users

   - Visual feedback for AI speaking (not just audio)

   - Alternative chat interface option

✅ **Feel modern** — 2025 web standards, not 2015 templates

✅ **Drive action** — Clear CTAs, minimal friction to start
